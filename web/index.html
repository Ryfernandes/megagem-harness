Meet the sampling bot ↗

Samples possible hidden hands and decks, then compares bids by playing out games with valuation bots. Each sampled world gets equal weight. Search has a time limit; short searches use the valuation policy.

Select “Sampling search · level 4”. Preparing a game takes longer; results can vary with available search time.

Meet the soft-tracking bot ↗

Matches the valuation bot at every seat count with all extras off. Select it to switch on hand inference, mission endgame rules, finance-aware cash planning, or a bid model that learns opponents' cash fractions for each auction type. Strategic reveals adapt to the chart: draw attention toward less useful colors on A/C, or try to cool competition for desired colors on B/D. E checks whether the reveal raises or lowers the displayed price. Cash planning uses remaining loans, investments, and treasure auctions to adjust future cash needs.

Select “Soft tracking · informed bids” in any seat to try it.

Meet the valuation bot ↗

Prices gems using expected final values and adds mission and denial value. Uses expected-utility bids with all seat counts, balancing win probability, price, and future cash needs. Keeps a cash reserve and weighs investments and loans against future buying power.

Select “Valuation · expected value” in any seat to try it.

Meet the fixed-rule bot ↗

20% of cash on treasure, rounded down. Zero on loans and investments. Reveals its first hidden gem.

Missions are claimed automatically. A zero bid can still win a financial card.
